🎉 DEPLOY WEBSITES KE CLOUDFLARE PAGES 🎉
===========================================

Website sudah bersih & siap 100% untuk di-deploy ke Cloudflare Pages!
(Tampilan QR & IP lokal sudah dihapus sepenuhnya).

-------------------------------------------
CARA DEPLOY 1: TANPA CODING (Dashboard Cloudflare)
-------------------------------------------
1. Buka https://dash.cloudflare.com/
2. Pilih menu "Workers & Pages" di sebelah kiri.
3. Klik "Create application" -> tab "Pages" -> pilih "Upload assets".
4. Ketik Nama Project: misal "sekar-birthday".
5. Drag & Drop folder /home/kacungsocrates/Documents/web_HB_Sekar
6. Klik "Deploy".
7. SELESAI! Website langsung aktif di https://sekar-birthday.pages.dev 💖

-------------------------------------------
CARA DEPLOY 2: VIA TERMINAL (Wrangler CLI)
-------------------------------------------
Jalankan di terminal:
  cd /home/kacungsocrates/Documents/web_HB_Sekar
  npx wrangler login
  npx wrangler pages deploy . --project-name=sekar-birthday
